I was sixteen when someone at the McDonald's down the street decided I could be trusted with the back drive thru. I did not know much about leadership, but someone gave me responsibility, trusted me with people and a process, and expected me to figure some things out.

A lot has happened since then. I have led in law enforcement, churches, ministries, teams, and organizations, while also learning what it means to be a husband and father. The titles have changed and the responsibility has grown, but after all these years I still feel like I have only scratched the surface.

Most of what I know about leadership has been learned while doing it, through successes I am grateful for and failures I would rather not repeat.

> The people who make up the Church that he entrusts me to lead bear the fruit of my ability to follow him.

That conviction keeps changing the questions I ask about leadership. Before asking how to get people somewhere, I have to ask whether I am actually following Jesus and whether I recognize where he is leading.

Some of the people who have helped me most were simply a few short steps ahead and willing to turn around and tell me what they were learning. That is what this section is for.

I hope this helps. If not, just share it with somebody you think it might benefit.
