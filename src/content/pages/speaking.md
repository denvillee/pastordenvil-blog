Most of my teaching lives around Scripture, the kingdom of God, formation, leadership, and the ordinary work of following Jesus. If you are planning a church gathering, retreat, staff conversation, or leadership environment and think I may be helpful, tell me what you are building.

Give me the shape of the room, who will be there, and what you are hoping for. I will tell you honestly whether I am the right voice for it.

If you are reaching out about coaching, use the same form and tell me what kind of leadership help you are looking for.
