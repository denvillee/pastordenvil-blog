passageText: "And immediately he saw the heavens being torn open and the Spirit descending on him like a dove. And a voice came from heaven: You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased. The Spirit immediately drove him out into the wilderness."
scene: "The water is still running off Him. His hair is wet. The voice has barely finished saying beloved, and the same Spirit that came down like a dove puts a hand on His back and pushes Him into the desert. Not leads. Drives. Mark uses a violent word and does not apologize for it. Forty days, wild animals, no bread. The affirmation and the wilderness are separated by one verse and no explanation at all."
response: "Where have you assumed that the wilderness meant the voice was wrong about you? Sit with the order of events. The belovedness came first. It came before the test, not as a reward for passing it."
summary: "The affirmation and the wilderness are one verse apart, and the Spirit does the pushing."
