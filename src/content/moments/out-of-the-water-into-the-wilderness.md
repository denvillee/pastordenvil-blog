## The Scripture

> At once the Spirit sent him out into the wilderness.
> <cite>Mark 1:12</cite>

## The Scene

The water is still running off him when it happens. The voice has barely finished. Beloved. Well pleased. And then the same Spirit that came down like a dove drives him out into a place with no water at all.

## The Response

Father, when the affirmation and the wilderness arrive in the same week, let me not read the second one as the withdrawal of the first.
