passageText: "When she could hide him no longer, she took for him a basket made of bulrushes and daubed it with bitumen and pitch. She put the child in it and placed it among the reeds by the river bank."
scene: "She waterproofs it herself. Bitumen on the outside, pitch on the inside, the same word the flood story uses for Noah's ark. A mother building the smallest ark in Scripture out of river grass, for one passenger, because the alternative is a soldier at the door. Then she walks away and leaves her son in the current, and stations his sister close enough to watch and too far to help."
response: "Name the thing you have had to set down in the water because holding it was no longer possible. Then notice who Scripture says was watching it."
