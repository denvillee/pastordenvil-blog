Genesis 1 comes before Genesis 3.

That matters.

God's first posture toward humanity is not rescue. It is love. Before sin enters
the story, before shame, before judgment, before the need for salvation,
humanity is made in the image of God. We were created by love and for communion
with God.

That is why John 3:16 matters so much. The first operative word is not gave. It
is loved.

> "For God so loved the world, that he gave..."

The giving of the Son does not create the love of God. It reveals it. Love is
not the result of salvation. Salvation is the expression of love.

I have seen saving happen for a lot of reasons. As a police officer, I saw how
someone can save out of duty, compassion, moral decency, or even instinct.
Sometimes people save because it is their job. Sometimes they save because they
want to do the right thing. Sometimes people even "save" in ways that are
tangled up with ego or self-righteousness.

But that is not what God is doing.

God is not saving us out of bare obligation. He is not reluctantly stepping in
because the situation got out of hand. He is acting from a love that was already
there. A predetermined, devoted, covenantal love. He loved, and so he gave. He
gave, and so he saved.

Salvation is not God becoming loving. It is God's love moving toward us in the
most costly way possible.
