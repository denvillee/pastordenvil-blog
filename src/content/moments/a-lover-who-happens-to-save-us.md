The power of God's love is not that he gets us out of every ditch we fall into.
The power of his love is found in the fact that he comes into the ditch with us.

Sometimes God doesn't get us out of every valley just so he could get into it
with us. Just so you would know how deep his love is. His love is valley deep.

The coming of Christ in history demonstrates that God is not afraid to get into
your mess.
