The first recorded sentence of Jesus' public ministry is not an invitation. It is a bulletin.

The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand. Repent, and believe the good news.

Read it slowly and notice that three of the four clauses are announcements and only one is a request. Something has happened. Something has arrived. Given that, change your mind and act accordingly.

We have made the kingdom into a mood. A sense of God's nearness that some people seem to have and others do not. But the word Jesus uses is a political word, and the phrase at hand is a spatial one. He is not describing an atmosphere. He is telling you where you are standing.
