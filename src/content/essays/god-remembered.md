There is a sentence at the end of Exodus 2 that does all the work in the book, and most of us read past it because nothing visible happens in it.

God heard their groaning, and God remembered His covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. God saw the people of Israel, and God knew.

Four verbs. Heard, remembered, saw, knew. Not one of them is rescued.
