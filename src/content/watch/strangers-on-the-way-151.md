Lauren Hansen's show is built on a single idea: that loneliness gets undone one
stranger at a time. In this conversation Denvil traces a working life that keeps
turning back on itself, from a first job taking orders at a drive thru to
leadership in retail, then a police badge and years as an investigator and
detective, and finally the work he does now.
