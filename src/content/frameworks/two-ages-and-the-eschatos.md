The top two bands are the standard picture. This age runs from creation to the renewal of all things. The age to come broke in early at the resurrection and has not finished arriving. We live in the overlap, which is why the church is never as healed, as unified, or as finished as we want it to be, and also why anything gets healed at all.

The third band is the correction. The end is not a date at the far right of the line. It is a person running underneath the whole of it: Christ **for us** at the cross, **in us** by the Spirit, **with us** at his coming.

Most weeks you need one of those three more than the other two.
